The spirit of Navratri

Nine nights of rhythm,
devotion and company

Navratri is a festival of devotion expressed through music, circling dance, and shared colour. At SUAS, it brings students, faculty and staff out of classrooms and into one ring, stepping to the same beat.

Each day carries its own colour and its own group. Mornings begin with aarti; afternoons belong to Garba and Dandiya. Come in the colour of the day.

01 / THINKTANK 2026

Two ways in.
One day on campus.

ThinkTank 2026 is hosted by ACM SUAS on 15 October 2026 at SUAS Amphitheatre. Bring an idea to Track A or an act to Track B. Entry is free, and participation is for SUAS students.

ThinkTank is its own programme. It is separate from the daily Navratri aarti and Garba schedule above.
